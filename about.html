About Our Institute

A house of creative craft.

For eighteen years ATELIER ÉLAN has taught fashion as both an art and a trade.

Our Story

Founded on a single studio table.

ATELIER ÉLAN began as a small Paris studio where a handful of designers taught what they knew: how to draw an idea, cut it, and make it real. That belief still shapes every class.

  1. 2008

    The first atelier opens in Paris with a single cohort.

  2. 2014

    The first graduate runway show is staged by students.

  3. 2019

    A second studio in Milan brings textile and print into the programme.

  4. Today

    More than 1,200 alumni design, illustrate and make worldwide.

Vision & Mission

We teach fashion as a language.

Our Vision

A world where every designer finds a voice that is unmistakably their own.

We see creativity as a discipline that can be taught, practised and refined.

Our Mission

To train designers who can imagine boldly and make with precision.

Through studio practice, mentorship and real briefs, we turn ideas into finished work.

  • Learn by making
  • Think like a designer
  • Work like a professional
Learning Approach

Learn in the studio, not only the classroom.

Three habits run through every programme.

Practical fashion training

Cutting tables and sewing rooms are open from the first week. You learn each skill by using it.

Creative development

Sketchbook reviews and critiques build a point of view.

Industry-oriented learning

Live briefs and guest designers keep the work close to the profession.

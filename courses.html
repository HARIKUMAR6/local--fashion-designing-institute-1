Programmes

Our courses

Four programmes, from first sketch to finished collection.

The Curriculum

Choose your creative direction.

Every programme is taught in the studio, around real briefs and finished work. Start with the foundation, go further with an advanced collection, or focus on a single craft: illustration or textiles.

  2. 02Advanced Fashion Designing18 Months

Fashion Design

From first drape to signature.

Two programmes for designers: a complete foundation, then an advanced collection of your own.

01

Diploma in Fashion Designing

A complete foundation in design, pattern cutting and garment construction, built around a graduate collection.

Duration
12 Months
Course overview
The foundation programme of the institute. It carries you from first sketch to finished garment, bringing design development, pattern cutting and construction together in one graduate collection.
Skills covered
  • Design development
  • Pattern cutting
  • Garment construction
  • Graduate collection
Enquire Now
02

Advanced Fashion Designing

Develop a signature collection with couture techniques, styling and portfolio direction.

Duration
18 Months
Course overview
For designers ready to go further. The programme concentrates on building a signature collection, refining couture technique and shaping a portfolio with direction and a point of view.
Skills covered
  • Signature collection
  • Couture techniques
  • Styling
  • Portfolio direction
Enquire Now
Textile & Fashion Styling

Colour, surface and image.

Fabric and the fashion image: print, weave and surface on one side, the drawn figure on the other. Styling itself is taught within Advanced Fashion Designing.

04

Textile Designing

Explore print, surface and weave, and turn colour and pattern into fabric with a voice of its own.

Duration
9 Months
Skills covered
  • Print
  • Surface
  • Weave
  • Colour & pattern
Course overview
A studio course in making cloth with intent. You work with colour, pattern and surface to develop fabrics that can carry a collection, from the first palette to the finished swatch.
Enquire Now
03

Fashion Illustration

Learn to draw the figure, the fabric and the idea, by hand and in digital media.

Duration
6 Months
Course overview
A focused course in the fashion image. You learn to draw the figure, describe fabric on paper and communicate a design idea clearly, working both by hand and digitally.
Skills covered
  • Figure drawing
  • Fabric rendering
  • Communicating ideas
  • Hand & digital media
Enquire Now

Practical Learning

Learn by making.

Cutting tables, sewing rooms and a print lab are open from the first week, so every course is learned with the hands.

  • Studio briefs

    Projects are set as briefs and reviewed in critique, close to how the industry works.

  • Cutting & construction

    Pattern tables and sewing machines for turning drawings into garments.

  • Mentorship & portfolio

    A personal mentor follows your progress, and each term adds finished work to a reviewed portfolio.

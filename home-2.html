The People Behind the Creativity

Every designer has a story to tell.

Q — What changed for you here?

“I learned to finish what I start. My graduate collection was the first thing I ever completed from sketch to stage.”

Amélie Laurent · Diploma in Fashion Designing

Q — What did your mentor teach you?

“To edit. Cutting the idea down to what matters made my collection sharper and entirely my own.”

Noor Hassan · Advanced Fashion Designing

Q — What are you making now?

“Illustrated prints for a small label. The drawing habit I built here is now my whole practice.”

Mia Kovač · Fashion Illustration

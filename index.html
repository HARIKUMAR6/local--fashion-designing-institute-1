01 · The Institute

More than an institute. A creative movement.

ATELIER ÉLAN is a studio-led school where design is learned with the hands as much as the eye. Small cohorts, working ateliers and mentors from the industry shape every student's point of view.

From the first sketch to the final fitting, you build a body of work that carries your name.

05 · Why ATELIER ÉLAN

Shaping the next generation of designers.

  1. 01

    Industry-focused learning

    Briefs, critiques and guest sessions led by working designers keep every project close to how the industry actually operates.

  2. 02

    Practical studio experience

    Cutting tables, sewing rooms and a print lab are open to you from the first week, so you learn by making.

  3. 03

    Portfolio development

    Each term adds finished pieces to a portfolio that is reviewed, edited and presented the way admissions and employers expect.

  4. 04

    Creative mentorship

    A personal mentor follows your progress, challenges your choices and helps you find a signature that is unmistakably yours.
